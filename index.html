Per 100g support

Per 100g is a fast, private calorie and macro tracker for iPhone, built around barcode scanning. Need help, found a mistake or have an idea? Email spike.sensor9m@icloud.com. I usually reply within a few days.

Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the app version (Settings > About in the app). Do not send your food diary unless I ask for it.

Privacy policy

Common questions

The scanner does not recognise a barcode.

Hold the phone steady about 15 to 20 cm from the pack, in good light, with the barcode flat and filling a good part of the frame. On a curved tin or bottle, turn it until the lines look straight. You can always choose "Enter barcode manually" and type the digits.

The camera says it is not allowed.

Open the iPhone's Settings app, choose Per 100g, and turn Camera on. Or type the barcode instead; the app works without the camera.

"We couldn't find this product."

Per 100g uses Open Food Facts, a free database built by volunteers, so some products are missing, especially new or small-brand ones. Choose "Add it manually" and enter the per 100 g values from the label. The food is saved for next time. You can also add the product to Open Food Facts to help everyone.

The numbers look wrong.

Product data can contain mistakes. Check the label on the pack. You can add the product yourself with the right numbers using "Add it manually", and a custom food with the same barcode is used in place of the database entry.

"Lookups are busy."

Open Food Facts limits how fast products can be looked up. Wait a moment and tap Try again.

Why is carbohydrate not reduced by fibre?

UK and EU labels already leave fibre out of the carbohydrate figure, so the number shown is effectively net carbs. The app does not subtract fibre again.

What is the difference between "of target" and "of calories"?

"Of calories" is how much of a food's (or your target's) calories come from protein, carbs or fat. "Of target" is how far through your daily target you are. If you set your targets as percentages, the app uses "of target" for your progress and "of calories" only when setting targets or describing a food.

Can I export my data, or delete it?

Yes. In Settings, choose Export data for CSV or JSON, or Delete all data to remove everything from the app. See the privacy policy for what that means for backups.

Does the app work offline?

Yes, for foods you have already scanned or added. A new barcode needs an internet connection for the lookup.

Is Per 100g medical advice?

No. It is a tracking tool. Speak to a doctor or dietitian before making big changes to your diet.
