Per 100g support

Privacy policy

Per 100g for iPhone. Last updated 31 October 2026.

In short

Everything you log in Per 100g stays on your iPhone, and in your own device backups if you use them. It is never sent to me. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only thing that leaves your phone is a barcode number, sent to Open Food Facts when you look up a product you have not scanned before.

Who I am

Per 100g is made by Mahir El-Shetob, an individual developer in the United Kingdom. Because I never receive your data, I do not hold personal data about you. You can contact me at spike.sensor9m@icloud.com.

What the app stores, and where

Per 100g stores the following on your iPhone only:

  • the foods you log (name, amount, meal, time and nutrition values);
  • foods you add yourself, your favourites and recently used foods;
  • your daily targets and your choice of grams or percentage targets;
  • products you have looked up, kept so they work again without an internet connection.

Diet information can be sensitive, so it is kept on the device and is not shared with anyone. I cannot see it. If you use iCloud Backup or an iTunes/Finder backup, your phone's backup can include this data; that is controlled by you and Apple, not by me, and is covered by Apple's privacy terms.

What leaves your phone

Barcode numberWhen you scan or type a barcode for a product the app does not already have, the app asks Open Food Facts (a free, open food database) for that product over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. The request contains the barcode number, and a User-Agent that names the app, its version and my contact email, as Open Food Facts requires. It contains no account, name or device identifier. Like any website, Open Food Facts can see your internet address (IP address) when you connect. Their own privacy policy applies to what they do with it.
Files you exportIf you choose Export in Settings, the app makes a CSV or JSON file and opens the iOS share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it. The app deletes its temporary copy when you close the sheet.

Nothing else is sent. I do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or any third-party code in the app.

Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes, and only after you tap to scan and allow access. Reading happens on your iPhone. Pictures are not saved and not sent anywhere. If you do not allow the camera, you can type the barcode instead. You can change the permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Your choices and rights

  • Export: Settings > Export data gives you everything you have logged as CSV or JSON.
  • Delete: Settings > Delete all data removes everything from the app and returns it to the first-run screen. Deleting the app also removes its data from your phone. A copy already in a device backup stays there until a newer backup replaces it, or until you delete that backup in your Apple account settings.
  • Because I do not receive or hold your data, there is nothing for me to access, correct or erase on my side. If you would like to ask me anything, use the email above.
  • If you are in the UK and have a concern about data protection, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Per 100g was not designed for young children. Counting calories is not suitable for everyone, so if a young person wants to use it, a parent or carer should decide whether it is right for them. I do not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever collects or shares anything new, I will update this page and the in-app About screen before the change is released, and the date above will change.

Product data and your health

Product data comes from Open Food Facts and can contain mistakes. Per 100g is a tracking tool, not medical advice.
